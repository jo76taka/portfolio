About:

Hi, I’m Josuke Takashima!

Problem solving is my passion. Design and data science help me find people’s real needs faster: interviews show me what a few people struggle with and data shows how many share it, while patterns in data tell me who to go and talk to. I fell for design after reading Tim Brown’s Change by Design, and now it’s part of my everyday life. I can empathize with people on a much deeper level, whether they’re my interviewee or my best friend.

That approach is behind the app my friends and I are building to help people manage their schedules without the stress!

I hope to keep combining design and data science to tackle the social issues that affect us all.

Design Philosophy:

Design and data science work in both directions. Talking with a few people reveals a real issue, and data shows how many people share it. Data reveals a pattern, and talking with people explains why. Together they get to the real issue faster. I look past individual problems to the structures that create them.
