Elementals

A luxury fragrance service where scents are unlocked by real places, not bought off a shelf.

Hero: Elementals app unlock screen + bottle

Elementals turns fragrance from something you buy into a memory you discover: each scent is earned through an experience in nature, then kept as a personal archive.

The Problem:

Premium in Price, Generic in Experience

Luxury fragrance shopping has barely changed in decades: walk into a store, smell a few blotter strips, buy a bottle. In a market crowded with similar launches, another bottle doesn't create new emotional value.

Insight:

Luxury Can Be Earned, Not Just Paid For

We were new to the European fragrance market, so we started by learning the traditional in-store ritual. The gap we found: nothing ties a scent to the places and moments it is meant to evoke. For younger consumers, a fragrance linked to a real memory is worth more than a higher price tag.

Service Design:

Discover, Unlock, Collect

The whole journey runs on a mobile phone. Its built-in sensors (sound, light, and GPS) check when real-world conditions are met.

01 UnlockNocturne

Find a quiet place after sunset to unlock this scent, then buy it in the app.

02 CollectGallery

Every unlock is saved with the place and time it happened.

03 Special editionsFujisan

Rare scents unlocked only at unique sites, such as Mt. Fuji.

Why It Works:

Sustainable, Feasible, Scalable

Sustainable

A refillable bottle system puts reuse ahead of replacement.

Feasible

Unlocking needs only a phone, and the fragrances are still developed and produced the usual way.

Scalable

Partnerships with national parks, cultural landmarks, and conservation groups add region-specific unlocks. These partnerships drive tourism and raise environmental awareness.

Outcome & Reflection:

Moving Fast in Unfamiliar Territory
